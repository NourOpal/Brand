Example: bedtime rewards

Phone down by 10 PM,
every night for a week.

1

A parent sets a bedtime rule in Opal Family, and a $10 reward from their Cash App for keeping all 7 nights.

2

The teen sees the goal and what they can earn, and accepts.

3

Opal checks each night that Instagram, Facebook and other apps stay closed after 10 PM.

4

The reward lands in the teen’s Cash App account.
